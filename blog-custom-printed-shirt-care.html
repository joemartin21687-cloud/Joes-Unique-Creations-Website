A Simple Care Routine for Your Custom Printed Shirts

Once your custom shirts are finished, the next part happens at home: wearing them, washing them and putting them away for next time. Whether the design represents your business, your team or a family memory, it is worth keeping the care instructions somewhere easy to find.

At Joe’s Unique Creations in Glenside, Pennsylvania, my basic care guidance is straightforward: turn printed garments inside out, wash in cold water and air dry when possible. Here is how to build those instructions into a simple routine without having to rethink every load of laundry.

Keep the instructions with the order

Before the shirts are handed out, make sure the person doing the washing has the care information. For a family order, that might mean saving the instructions on your phone. For a business or team order, share them with everyone receiving a garment rather than keeping the information only with the organizer.

You can always return to the apparel care and order terms page for the shop’s current instructions. If a particular item comes with additional guidance, keep that information too. The garment’s own care label matters, especially when its instructions are more restrictive.

Give a new order time before its first wash

My posted instructions ask customers to wait twenty-four hours after delivery before washing. Plan around that when you collect your order, particularly if the shirts are intended for an event soon afterward. If you have questions about timing, bring them up when pickup or delivery is being arranged.

Take a moment to inspect the order before putting it into your normal laundry routine. If something appears wrong at pickup or delivery, contact me before washing, wearing, altering or attempting to repair the item. Clear photos and your order information help explain what you are seeing.

Turn the garment inside out

Make turning the garment inside out part of preparing the load. It is a small step that can be easy to forget when printed shirts are mixed with everyday clothing. Check the front and back before the item goes into the machine so the printed side is facing inward.

If several people share the laundry, explain the routine to them as well. A team shirt or work hoodie may pass through more than one person’s hands between being worn and being washed. Keeping the instructions simple makes them easier for everyone to follow consistently.

Use the washing settings in the care guidance

For printed apparel from the shop, use cold water on a gentle cycle with mild detergent. Do not use bleach or fabric softener. Check the products you are adding to the load rather than assuming the settings alone cover every part of the care instructions.

Read the garment label before washing, and follow it if it calls for more restrictive care. Different garments may have different requirements. If you are unsure how the printed design guidance and the garment label fit together, ask before choosing a stronger treatment or changing the routine.

Make air drying your first choice

My preferred instruction is to air dry when possible. Leave enough time for that when planning laundry before a workday, school activity or family event. Having the garment ready ahead of time helps you avoid reaching for a quicker drying method without checking the instructions.

The posted care page says to use low heat if a dryer is used. Do not iron directly over the design. If the garment needs attention beyond the usual washing routine, review the care label and ask about the item instead of experimenting directly on the printed area.

Share the routine with your group

For school and team apparel, a short care message sent with the distribution details can be useful. For company shirts, keep the same information with employee apparel records. The person who placed the order may know the instructions, but the person wearing the shirt needs them too.

Keep the message practical: wait before the first wash, turn inside out, use cold water and gentle settings, skip bleach and fabric softener, and air dry when possible. Refer people to the full care page for the remaining details. A consistent routine gives your custom apparel the care you agreed to when ordering and makes the next laundry day easier to manage.
