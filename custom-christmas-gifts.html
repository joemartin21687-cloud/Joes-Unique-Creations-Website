Christmas gift questions

Can you use my own photo for a Christmas gift?

Yes. Submit your photo with your order request. Joe reviews image quality and placement for the selected product before confirming the order.

How much do personalized Christmas gifts cost?

Starting prices range from $5 for photo magnets and custom pens to $35 for a small photo crystal. Product size, blank style and extra design work affect the final total.

Do you offer Christmas gifts for Philadelphia customers?

Yes. Joe’s Unique Creations is based in Glenside and serves customers in Philadelphia and Montgomery County. Contact Joe to confirm timing and fulfillment arrangements.

When should I order for Christmas?

Order early, especially for specialty blanks or group orders. Include your needed-by date; Joe confirms availability and completion timing before payment.
