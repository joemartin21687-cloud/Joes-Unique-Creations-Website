Custom Apparel Care, Customer-Supplied Garments & Order Terms

These terms explain how to prepare artwork, care for printed apparel, and report a possible production issue. The order form asks you to acknowledge them before submitting your order request. Joe will confirm availability, timing, and final price before production and payment.

Customer-Supplied Clothing — No Warranty or Replacement for Garment-Related Issues

Customers who provide garments are responsible for choosing the correct size, fit, color, and garment type, and for checking each item’s condition before drop-off. Please point out any stains, wear, defects, or special fabric treatments. Customer-supplied garments may react differently to the heat and pressure used in printing.

Customer-supplied clothing is customized at the customer’s risk for unforeseen garment-related issues outside our control. These may include hidden defects, fabric composition, coatings or treatments, prior wear or washing, dye migration, shrinkage, heat sensitivity, and problems with print adhesion caused by the garment. For issues caused by these garment conditions, Joe’s Unique Creations provides no warranty, refunds, free repairs, or replacements for the supplied clothing or its customization. This applies to issues that occur during production or appear after delivery, including peeling, cracking, discoloration, or other damage caused by the garment. We will use reasonable care while handling customer property. This policy does not exclude responsibility for defects in our own workmanship or remove rights or remedies that cannot legally be limited.

Fine Lines and Small Details

Very thin lines, tiny text, distressed effects, and other small details provide less surface area for adhesion and may be more vulnerable to lifting or wear. Customers must review and approve artwork before production. If durability is a priority, ask us to thicken or simplify fine details before approving the design.

Order Details and Artwork Approval

Before approving production, review spelling, sizes, colors, quantities, garment choices, artwork, and print placement. Production begins after approval. Screen and garment colors can look different under different lighting and on different displays. Custom items cannot be returned because of a change in preference or an error included in the customer-approved order.

Washing and Care

Damage caused by improper care is not covered. Joe’s Unique Creations is not responsible for peeling, cracking, lifting, fading, or other damage caused by washing or drying contrary to the care instructions below. No refunds, warranty coverage, free repairs, or replacements are provided for damage caused by improper care after delivery, including hot-water washing, washing without turning the garment inside out, or high-heat drying. This applies to both customer-supplied clothing and clothing supplied by Joe’s Unique Creations.

  • Wait 24 hours after delivery before washing.
  • Turn the garment inside out and wash in cold water on a gentle cycle with mild detergent.
  • Do not use bleach or fabric softener.
  • Air dry when possible. If using a dryer, use low heat.
  • Do not iron directly over the design.
  • Follow the garment’s care label if it is more restrictive.

Reporting a Possible Production Issue

Inspect your order at pickup or delivery. Contact us in writing within 3 calendar days if you believe there is a production issue. Include your order number and clear photos. Do not wash, wear, alter, or attempt to repair the item before contacting us.

We will review the issue and, if a defect in our work is confirmed, provide an appropriate remedy as required by law. Normal wear, improper care, misuse, and defects in a customer-supplied garment are not defects in our printing work.
