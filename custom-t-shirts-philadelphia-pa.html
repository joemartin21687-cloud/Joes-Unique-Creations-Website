PHILADELPHIA CUSTOM SHIRT PRINTING

Local service without the large online-print-shop runaround

Our production shop is located at 745 Edge Hill Road in Glenside, just outside Philadelphia. Customers work directly with Joe on artwork, garment choices, placement, quantities and deadlines. We handle one-of-a-kind shirts, small group orders and larger production runs.

DTF printing is our primary apparel process because it supports detailed logos, photographs, gradients and full-color artwork on many compatible cotton, polyester and blended garments. Designs can be printed on light or dark shirts and placed on the front, back, left chest or sleeves.
