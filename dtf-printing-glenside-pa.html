DIRECT-TO-FILM PRINTING

Full-color DTF printing for detailed custom apparel

DTF, short for direct-to-film, is our primary apparel-printing method. Artwork is printed in full color onto transfer film, coated with adhesive powder, cured and then heat pressed onto a compatible garment. The process is a strong fit for colorful logos, photographs, gradients, names, numbers and artwork with fine detail.

Because DTF uses a white underbase, it can reproduce bright full-color graphics on many light and dark garments. We use it for custom T-shirts, hoodies, business apparel, work shirts, school and team clothing, memorial shirts, event apparel and other personalized garments.

WHY CUSTOMERS CHOOSE DTF

DTF handles color, detail and flexible order sizes well

DTF is useful when an order has multiple colors or detailed artwork because the design does not need to be separated into individual screen-printing colors. It can also make sense for smaller quantities and jobs with several different graphics, provided the artwork and garments are suitable for the process.

For the cleanest result, send the best artwork available. A high-resolution transparent PNG works well for many jobs, while vector files are especially useful for logos and text. Our artwork and file preparation guide explains PNG, JPG, SVG, PDF and vector files in more detail.
