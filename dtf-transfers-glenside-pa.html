WHAT YOU CAN ORDER

DTF transfers for one design, repeat jobs and larger runs.

DTF stands for Direct-to-Film. Your design is printed in full color with a white ink layer, adhesive is applied and cured, and the finished transfer is supplied ready for heat-press application. This makes DTF useful for colorful logos, photos, gradients and detailed artwork that would be difficult to reproduce with traditional cut vinyl.

Business & Logo Transfers

Keep transfers on hand for employee shirts, contractor workwear, uniforms, promotional apparel and repeat company orders.

Photos & Detailed Artwork

Full-color DTF can reproduce photographs, gradients, fine details and multicolor graphics when the source artwork is clean and high resolution.

Light & Dark Garments

The white underbase used in DTF allows designs to stay bright on many compatible light and dark cotton, polyester and blended garments.

Small Runs & Bulk Quantities

Order what your project requires. Transfer pricing is based on dimensions, quantity, artwork preparation and the specifics of the job.

ARTWORK THAT PRINTS WELL

Send the cleanest file you have.

For best results, send artwork at the intended print size whenever possible. High-resolution transparent PNG files work well for many DTF jobs, while SVG, EPS, AI and PDF vector files are especially helpful for logos and text. If all you have is a photo, screenshot or older logo file, send it with your quote request and we can review whether cleanup or recreation is needed before printing.

Transparent Backgrounds

If the design is not supposed to print as a rectangle, artwork should have a transparent background or be prepared for background removal.

Correct Print Size

Tell us the finished width or intended placement so the transfer can be sized correctly for left chest, full front, full back, sleeve or another location.

Color & Detail Review

Fine lines, tiny text and low-resolution images are checked before production so obvious artwork problems can be addressed before transfers are printed.

HOW TO ORDER

DTF TRANSFER FAQ

Common questions about ready-to-press transfers.

What is a ready-to-press DTF transfer?

It is a finished full-color transfer that you apply to a compatible garment with a heat press. We handle the DTF printing and curing; you handle the final press.

Can DTF transfers work on black shirts?

Yes. The white ink underbase makes DTF suitable for many dark compatible garments as well as light garments.

Do you print transfers in bulk?

Yes. We quote both small and larger transfer orders. Send the dimensions, artwork and quantities for accurate pricing.
