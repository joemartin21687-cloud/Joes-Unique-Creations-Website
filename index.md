# Joe’s Unique Creations

[Main website](https://joesuniquecreationspa.com/)

Joe prints custom T-shirts, hoodies and personalized gifts in Glenside, Pennsylvania, serving Montgomery County and the Philadelphia area. Standard custom T-shirts start at $18 with no minimum, and standard hoodies start at $45.

## Ordering and pickup

Call or text Joe at 610-368-0865 from 8 AM to 7 PM daily, or [request a price](https://form.jotform.com/262881102440045). Send your idea, quantity and needed-by date. Joe confirms the design, price and timing before payment. Contact Joe to confirm pickup at 745 Edge Hill Road, Glenside, PA 19038.

Qualifying same-day orders of up to 12 standard T-shirts may be available when ordered by 10 AM, subject to stock, approved artwork and production capacity. Gildan Hammer items must be ordered and are unavailable for same-day printing.
