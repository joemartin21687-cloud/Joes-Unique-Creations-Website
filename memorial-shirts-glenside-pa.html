PHOTO QUALITY MATTERS

Send the original image whenever possible.

A memorial print can only reproduce detail that exists in the source photo. The best files are original phone-camera images, original digital photos or high-resolution scans. Social-media screenshots, tiny thumbnails and heavily compressed images can look soft or pixelated when enlarged for a shirt.

Best

Original photo files, high-resolution scans and clear images where the person's face is not cropped or blurred.

Usable After Review

Older photos, busy backgrounds and images that may need cleanup, background removal or careful cropping.

Avoid When Possible

Small screenshots, photos downloaded repeatedly from social media and images with heavy blur or compression.

WHAT TO INCLUDE

Send everything together so the design can be prepared accurately.

When requesting a quote, include the photo or photos, exact spelling of the name, dates, wording, preferred colors, shirt color, quantity by size, print location and deadline. If you have a specific example or layout idea, include that too. Artwork should be reviewed and approved before production when custom design changes are involved.
