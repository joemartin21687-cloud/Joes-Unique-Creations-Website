Christmas gift questions

Can I put a loved one’s photo on an ornament?

Yes. Family photos, memorial portraits and pet pictures can be printed on the suitable face or insert of the selected ornament.

Is the whole metal snowflake printed?

The standard metal snowflake style has a photo insert in its center. The decorative metal frame surrounds that printed image.

Can I order matching ornaments with different names?

Yes. Include the quantity and each exact name in your request. Joe confirms the layout, available blanks and final price.

Can ornaments have printing on both sides?

Some blanks support two-sided printing. The listed base prices include one printed face or insert; ask Joe for available two-sided options and pricing.
