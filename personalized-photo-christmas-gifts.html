Christmas gift questions

Are photo crystals engraved in 3D?

The offered photo crystals use a full-color printed photo on coated crystal glass. They are not 3D laser-engraved portraits.

Can I use a photo collage?

Yes. Send the photos and describe the layout you want. Basic photo placement is included; more involved collage or artwork work is quoted separately.

Can you print children’s artwork on gifts?

Yes. Send a clear scan or well-lit photo of the artwork and choose a compatible gift. Joe checks the file and printable area.

Is personalization included in the price?

The listed starting prices include the described standard blank and basic photo/name placement with one simple layout. Premium blanks, extra sides and major artwork changes are quoted before payment.
