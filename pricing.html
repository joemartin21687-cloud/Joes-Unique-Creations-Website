WHAT'S INCLUDED & WHAT CAN CHANGE THE PRICE

Know what you're paying for.

Standard Garments

Base prices use standard Gildan cotton T-shirts or standard Gildan pullover hoodies. Bella+Canvas, Comfort Colors, performance shirts, premium fleece and other garments are available for the price difference.

Print Locations

Base pricing assumes standard DTF decoration. Additional front, back or sleeve locations, unusually large artwork, specialty placement, names or numbers are quoted based on the job.

Extended Sizes

2XL and larger garments may cost more because blank garment costs are higher. We will show the difference before production.

Artwork

Print-ready artwork keeps pricing lowest. Major design work, recreation of low-resolution logos or complex artwork may require a design fee.

Same-Day Service

Qualifying orders of 12 T-shirts or fewer confirmed by 10 AM can be completed the same day when garments are available and artwork is ready or approved promptly.

Bulk Orders

Need 50, 100 or more pieces? Contact us. Large jobs can be quoted around garment choice, print locations and production requirements.
