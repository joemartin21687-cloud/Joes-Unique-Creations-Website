POPULAR QUESTIONS

What customers usually want to know first.

What file should I send?

A high-resolution transparent PNG works well for many DTF jobs. Vector files such as SVG, EPS, AI or PDF are especially useful for logos and text. See the artwork guide.

Is DTF good for full color?

Yes. DTF is especially useful for multi-color logos, photos, gradients and detailed artwork on many compatible light and dark garments. See the DTF guide.

How fast can shirts be made?

Qualifying orders of 12 shirts or fewer placed by 10:00 AM can be completed the same day when garments are available and artwork is ready and approved.

Do bigger orders cost less per shirt?

Our posted pricing uses quantity tiers. Final pricing also depends on garment choice, sizes, print locations, artwork and personalization.
